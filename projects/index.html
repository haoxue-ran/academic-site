Projects

My current work focuses on membrane fouling control, data-driven environmental modeling, and resource recovery.

research directions

Membrane Fouling Mechanisms

Quantifying interfacial interactions between foulants and membranes and developing prevention strategies for more stable filtration performance.

Machine Learning & Causal Inference

Using interpretable models such as CatBoost and XGBoost to decouple complex foulant-membrane interactions and support evidence-based treatment decisions.

Molecular Simulation

Applying multi-scale molecular dynamics simulations and density functional theory to gain microscopic insight into fouling and interfacial processes.

Resource Recovery

Studying interfacial crystallization mechanisms and developing novel strategies for selective lithium extraction from salt-lake brines.
