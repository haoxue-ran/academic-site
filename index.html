Haoxue Ran

Membrane-based water treatment, machine learning, and molecular simulation for environmental applications.

School of Environmental and Municipal Engineering,

Xi'an University of Architecture and Technology,

Xi'an, China

I am Haoxue Ran (冉浩学), a Ph.D. candidate in Environmental Science and Engineering at the School of Environmental and Municipal Engineering, Xi'an University of Architecture and Technology (XAUAT). I work in the research group of Prof. Rui Miao (苗瑞) and collaborate with Associate Researcher Jin Zhao (赵进) at Xi'an Jiaotong University. I expect to graduate in June 2027 and am actively seeking international postdoctoral positions in advanced water treatment and environmental data science.

My research lies at the intersection of membrane-based water treatment and data-driven frameworks. I use interpretable machine learning, causal inference, and molecular simulations to analyze organic membrane fouling mechanisms, and I also study interfacial crystallization for lithium recovery from salt-lake brine.
